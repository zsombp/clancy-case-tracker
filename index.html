            About this site

            This is an independent, non-commercial reference page. It is not affiliated with any court, party, law firm, or news organization. It has no ads, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics.

            How it stays current. A scheduled job runs every hour, pulls public news and video feeds, keeps only items that mention the case, removes duplicates, and appends them to an archive. The "updated" indicator at the top reflects the last successful run. The curated sections (status, timeline, people, legal, court) are reviewed and updated by hand and carry their own "curated as of" date so you can always tell the two layers apart.

            Objectivity. Lindsay Clancy is presumed innocent of the charges unless and until a jury returns a verdict, and this page uses "alleged" and "charged with" accordingly. Where the prosecution and defense disagree, both positions are described as what each side asserts, with sources. Nothing here is legal advice or medical advice.

            Sensitivity. This case involves the deaths of three young children. The page avoids graphic detail beyond what is necessary to understand the charges and the evidence described in open court.

            Corrections. If something is wrong or missing, open an issue on the repository with the entry, the problem, and a source.
